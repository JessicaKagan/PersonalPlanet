# Personal Planet

Heads up - this project is in pre-alpha! It'll become more complete and elaborate over time. The goal for _Personal Planet_ remains what it was in my initial planning/brainstorming document - an incremental game loosely inspired by Maxis' old "software toys", and SimEarth in particular.

_Personal Planet_, in the long run, is intended to be a game about societies, civilizations, and how they affect and are affected by the world they live in. The gameplay goals are going to revolve around cultivating your world, its life, its people, etc. Victory conditions could include growing a world that's full of diverse lifeforms and people, or uniting the world under a civilization with specific ethics and societal organization, or (for a more dark playthrough) creating a species capable of destroying itself and the world it exists in.

The incremental angle comes in here - your ability to modify the world is limited, and you need to complete various goals in-universe to grow your ability to act on it. Focus on specific tools for shaping the world, and you'll be able to do more on that front, potentially at the expense of your other means of planetary resculpting.

As of June 18th, 2026, this project is online more to document a personal project I'm working on as time and energy permit. I'm building from a Phaser/Svelte template project; see `TEMPLATE_README.md` for the current setup instructions. You can expect online hosted builds in the future, when I've had more time and energy to design and build out this toy.
